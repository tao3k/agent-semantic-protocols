* Ownership

This layer owns the mathematical exposition embedded as Org Babel Typst,
executable Lean models under =packages/proofs=, Lean JSON audit receipts, and
AXLE consumption of those receipts.

It does not own runtime search implementation. A proof failure or countermodel
is an RFC design input until the RFC model is repaired.

* Audit identity finding

The legacy merge and cache receipts contain more than one theorem in the same
theorem family. The standalone Lean-to-AXLE consumer originally rejected both
with =duplicate theorem family= even though the canonical AXLE validator
already treated theorem families as a coverage set. The standalone rule
confused a grouping dimension with an identity dimension.

The corrected mathematical contract is:

- =theoremId= MUST be unique within one receipt;
- =(familyId, obligationId)= MUST be unique within one receipt;
- =familyId= MAY repeat because one family may contain several obligations;
- required-family coverage means every required family has at least one
  accepted theorem, not exactly one theorem.

The executable countermodel is
[[file:../../../../packages/proofs/ASPProof/AuditFamilyIdentity.lean][ASPProof.AuditFamilyIdentity]].
Its axiom audit is
[[file:../../../../packages/proofs/ASPProof/Audit/AuditFamilyIdentity.lean][ASPProof.Audit.AuditFamilyIdentity]].
The modular Lean JSON emitter is
[[file:../../../../packages/proofs/audit/AuditFamilyIdentityAudit.lean][AuditFamilyIdentityAudit.lean]],
and its checked source receipt is
[[file:../../../../packages/proofs/receipts/audit-family-identity-audit-v1.json][audit-family-identity-audit-v1.json]].
The AXLE consumer accepts 2 declarations in the repeated
=audit-family-identity= family with receipt hash
=ce86f9b0d21a6c21c791f6cba512c7eb32d453c4ed04f2a49537236f9ce7d667=.
The standalone consumer now follows the canonical validator:

- allowed axioms are an upper bound rather than an exact inventory;
- required theorem families and RFC clauses are lower-bound coverage sets;
- emitted theorem families are canonicalized as a sorted set;
- =auditSha256= hashes canonical sorted compact JSON, not presentation bytes.

The merge receipt validates as 13 declarations across 5 theorem families with
audit hash
=4a64686fefd07261443ef4ec3c122d4dceaf57e6f4b378d37c8b3709af998d28=.
The cache receipt validates as 12 declarations across 5 theorem families with
audit hash
=bfa665421555ff0a3a6977160da5568bf71a25a2c0c2b7c5bb07806c7187d162=.
A successful Lean build still is not sufficient evidence by itself; AXLE
receipt validation remains a separate required gate.
