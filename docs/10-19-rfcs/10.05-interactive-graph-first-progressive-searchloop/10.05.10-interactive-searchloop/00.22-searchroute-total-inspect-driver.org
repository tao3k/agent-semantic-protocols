* Status

This RFC is the liveness layer above [[file:00.21-searchroute-repeated-inspect-loop.org][RFC 10.05.21 — SearchRoute Repeated Inspect Loop]].
RFC 10.05.21 proves safety, strict progress for every valid decision, coverage
conservation, and zero-work closure. This RFC specifies the additional premise
needed to prove that a driver can actually reach such a closed state.

This session does not authorize Rust implementation. The normative artifacts
are this Org RFC, its embedded Typst model, the Lean proof under
=packages/proofs=, and the Lean JSON plus AXLE audit receipts.

* Safety is not liveness

** Early-stop defect
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       ASP-RFC-10.05-SID-SAFETY-LIVENESS
:END:

The inductive =InspectLoopTrace= relation includes a zero-step =stop=
constructor. That constructor is correct for finite trace prefixes, but it can
stop at a state whose pending frontier is not empty.

Consequently the proposition “a trace exists” does not imply:

- the driver produced a next decision;
- the search closed;
- the candidate universe was exhausted or safely pruned; or
- the selected candidate is globally optimal.

Lean exhibits this defect directly: for every non-closed state, the zero-step
trace from the state to itself still exists. The protocol therefore needs a
typed =ClosedTraceReceipt= rather than an unqualified trace-existence claim.

* Total decision source

** Decision availability
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       ASP-RFC-10.05-SID-DECISION-TOTAL
:END:

A decision source is total when, for every well-formed non-closed loop state,
it produces at least one =LoopDecision= satisfying =DecisionValid=.

#+begin_example
DecisionTotal :=
  for every state:
    LoopWellFormed(state)
    and not LoopClosed(state)
    implies exists decision: DecisionValid(state, decision)
#+end_example

This premise is intentionally separate from decision validity:

- =DecisionValid= proves that one returned decision is semantically safe;
- =DecisionTotal= proves that the provider/driver does not become silent while
  certified work remains.

A timeout, missing parser capability, unavailable materialization owner, or
provider failure must therefore be represented as a typed terminal failure or
an explicit loss of totality. It must not be projected as a successful
zero-step trace.

The totality contract is semantic, not a requirement to expose another complex
CLI command. The agent-facing surface can remain one progressive =inspect=
operation whose receipt is either a valid decision or a typed failure.

* Constructive closed trace

** Strong-induction driver theorem
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       ASP-RFC-10.05-SID-CLOSED-TRACE
:END:

Let =W(state)= be the sum of positive pending-frontier credits from RFC
10.05.21. Lean constructs a =ClosedTraceReceipt= by strong induction on
=W(state)=:

1. If the state is already closed, return the zero-step closed receipt.
2. Otherwise, =DecisionTotal= supplies a valid decision.
3. =DecisionValid= and RFC 10.05.21 prove that the successor has strictly
   smaller work.
4. The induction hypothesis returns a closed receipt for that successor.
5. Prefixing the chosen decision returns a closed receipt for the original
   state.

The receipt contains:

- the final state;
- the exact number of loop decisions;
- the complete typed =InspectLoopTrace=; and
- proof that the final state is closed.

Every such receipt satisfies:

#+begin_example
receipt.steps <= loopWork(initial)
#+end_example

This is a liveness bound on agent/LLM interaction decisions. It is not yet a
proof that the driver uses the least graph hops, uncached tokens, rounds, or
transitions among all possible total drivers.

* Bounded global result

** Liveness plus conserved coverage
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       ASP-RFC-10.05-SID-GLOBAL
:END:

Assume:

- a total decision source;
- a well-formed initial state; and
- the snapshot-bound candidate-universe coverage invariant from RFC 10.05.21.

Lean then constructs a closed trace receipt satisfying all of the following:

- its length is bounded by the initial work;
- its final selected candidate is no worse than the initial selection; and
- its final selected candidate is no worse than every candidate in the bound
  universe.

This theorem closes the safety-to-liveness gap, but it remains conditional on
=DecisionTotal=. The proof does not manufacture parser availability,
materialization evidence, or provider health.

The theorem also does not establish *driver-cost optimality*. It proves that a
total driver reaches a globally optimal candidate, not that its trace is the
cheapest way to reach that candidate. The next proof layer must lift complete
inspect traces into =SearchRoute= cost candidates and compare graph hops,
uncached tokens, model/tool rounds, transitions, semantic-search cache effects,
and model prefix-cache effects without collapsing those axes.

= Safety relation

The finite trace relation permits a zero-step constructor:

$ S arrow.r.long S. $

Therefore trace existence alone does not imply $Closed(S)$. A non-closed state
can still have the trivial trace to itself.

= Total decision premise

A decision source is total when

$ forall S, quad Good(S) and not Closed(S)
  arrow.r exists d: Valid(S, d). $

Here $Good$ includes positive work credits and the loop invariants required by
the semantic decision rules.

= Work induction

Let

$ W(S) = sum_(F in P(S)) c(F). $

Every valid decision satisfies

$ Valid(S, d) arrow.r W(d(S)) < W(S). $

Strong induction on $W(S)$ constructs a final state $S_f$ and a trace of
$n$ decisions such that

$ S arrow.r.long S_f, quad Closed(S_f), quad n <= W(S). $

= Coverage and result

If $Cover(C, S)$ holds for the snapshot-bound candidate universe $C$, every
valid decision preserves it. Closure removes the pending alternative:

$ Closed(S_f) and Cover(C, S_f)
  arrow.r forall x in C: s_f prec.eq x. $

Thus totality plus semantic validity yields bounded closure and global
candidate optimality over $C$.

= Remaining optimization obligation

The liveness theorem minimizes no operational cost. Two total drivers can both
close within $W(S)$ while using different graph hops, uncached tokens,
model/tool rounds, transitions, search-cache reuse, and model prefix-cache
reuse. Complete traces must next be lifted into costed route candidates before
the protocol can claim the cheapest driver.

* Agent-facing result

The public interaction remains small:

#+begin_example
inspect(state)
  -> valid prune or inspect decision
  -> next state with lower work
  -> repeat
  -> closed receipt
#+end_example

The liveness contract belongs in receipts and provider capability evidence,
not in an expanded human-oriented search-command grammar.
