* Ownership

This layer owns the logical units that every SearchLoop implementation and
adapter must preserve: evidence obligations, bound-clause algebra, feasibility,
and the risk register for clause-first search.

It does not own online loop transitions, graph traversal policy, cache
identity, or proof execution.
