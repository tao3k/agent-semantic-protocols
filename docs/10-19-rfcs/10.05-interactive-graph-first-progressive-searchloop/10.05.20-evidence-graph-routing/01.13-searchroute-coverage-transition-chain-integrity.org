This RFC is canonical for reusing a Pareto vector-coverage certificate across
more than one graph generation.

* Lean-discovered composition gap

The single-transition predicate in the previous RFC requires only
=fromGeneration < toGeneration=. That condition proves monotonicity, but it
does not prove successor continuity.

Consequently, an otherwise authorized transition from generation 8 directly to
generation 10 satisfies the old predicate. Such a certificate can skip
generation 9, including a semantic change at generation 9 that was later
reverted at generation 10.

The repository already proves that a certified registry transition advances
exactly one epoch and that a certified transition chain cannot omit trust
edges. The missing logic was a bridge between that registry chain and the
semantic identity carried by a vector-coverage certificate.

The correction is therefore not another independent chain abstraction. Every
coverage transition edge MUST carry and align with one certified registry
transition edge.

* Mathematical model

Let =D= be the registry clock-domain type, =I= the semantic coverage-identity
type, and =S : D -> I= the semantic identity actually published at each clock
domain.

A certified coverage edge is a pair:

- a coverage transition =g=;
- a registry transition certificate =r=.

The edge is valid only when:

1. =r= is a valid authorized registry successor;
2. the generation endpoints of =g= equal the epochs of =r=;
3. the authority digests of =g=, =r=, and the serving context agree;
4. both =S(r.from)= and =S(r.to)= equal the coverage-header identity;
5. =g= is explicitly authorized.

A valid coverage chain is a connected list of such edges. Its registry
projection is therefore a valid registry transition chain. The existing
registry theorem then gives:

#+begin_example
start.epoch + edgeCount = end.epoch
#+end_example

This equation prevents a one-edge certificate from claiming a two-epoch
transition.

* Normative clauses

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-REGISTRY-BRIDGE

Every cross-generation coverage edge MUST carry a registry transition
certificate. Coverage generation endpoints, registry epochs, and authority
digests MUST agree exactly. A bare =GenerationTransition= is not sufficient
evidence for cross-generation reuse.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-SUCCESSOR-CONTINUITY

The registry projection of a coverage chain MUST be a valid certified
transition chain. Every edge advances exactly one certified epoch, and:

#+begin_example
startEpoch + edgeCount == endEpoch
#+end_example

Monotonic =fromGeneration < toGeneration= is necessary but insufficient.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-INTERMEDIATE-SEMANTIC

For every edge, the semantic lineage at both its source and destination domain
MUST equal the coverage-header semantic identity. Checking only the issued and
current endpoints is forbidden.

Any missing intermediate semantic identity, semantic mismatch, or unverified
intermediate domain rejects the complete chain.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-FORK-PREVENTION

The authorized registry-successor relation used by coverage reuse MUST be
functional. Two valid coverage edges with the same source domain MUST resolve
to the same destination domain.

A forked publication history cannot authorize cache or proof reuse until a
separate reconciliation proof selects one canonical successor.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-CHAIN-COMPOSITION

Two valid coverage-chain fragments may be appended only when the first fragment
ends at the exact clock domain where the second fragment begins. Composition
preserves every registry trust edge and every intermediate semantic check.

A summary or endpoint digest MUST NOT replace the underlying certified chain
unless an independently audited chain-compression proof binds the full edge
list.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-ENDPOINTS-INSUFFICIENT

Equal semantic identities at the issued and current generations do not imply
that all intermediate generations have the same identity. Endpoint equality
alone MUST NOT admit cross-generation coverage reuse.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CTCI-GLOBAL-LIFT

Only chain-admitted vector coverage may lift frontier-local Pareto minimality to
global Pareto minimality after more than one graph generation.

* Admission and failure behavior

The serving path MUST fail closed when:

- a coverage edge has no registry transition certificate;
- registry and coverage endpoints disagree;
- the chain omits an epoch;
- the successor relation is not functional;
- any intermediate semantic identity differs;
- an authority digest differs;
- the chain endpoints do not match the issued and current generations.

These failures invalidate proof reuse. They do not invalidate the underlying
source evidence; the SearchLoop may rebuild coverage under the current
generation.

* Explicit boundary

This RFC assumes one stable transition-authority digest across the admitted
coverage chain. Authority rotation requires a separate proof that composes
authority transfer with semantic-lineage continuity. It MUST NOT be inferred
from an epoch transition alone.
