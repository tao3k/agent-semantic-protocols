#+TITLE: Relation Postings and Graph Escalation
#+OPTIONS: toc:3

#+property: rfc_layer 10.05.20-evidence-graph-routing

* Purpose
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-PURPOSE
:END:

This module defines the physical retrieval boundary below bound evidence
clauses and the admitted graph boundary above them.  It prevents an index layout
from becoming an accidental logical or closure contract.

* Logical fact identity
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-FACT-IDENTITY
:END:

Each admitted relation fact has one stable identity and contains:

- relation kind and schema version;
- ordered role-labeled arguments;
- proof-relevant qualifiers;
- owner, workspace, project, and source generation;
- provider and parser artifact identity;
- provenance and trust scope;
- completeness and freshness class;
- exact source or external witness references where available.

Two postings that mention the same endpoints do not establish the same fact
unless they resolve to a compatible logical fact identity.

* Physical posting families
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-POSTINGS
:END:

Storage MAY factor a fact into indexes such as:

- relation-kind → fact identifiers;
- relation-kind + argument-role + entity → fact identifiers;
- owner or package → fact identifiers;
- selector → fact identifiers;
- context or qualifier → fact identifiers;
- source generation → fact identifiers;
- forward and inverse argument lookup;
- provider capability and completeness scope.

Posting values SHOULD remain compact fact identifiers plus bounded routing
metadata.  The full logical fact is loaded only for selected candidates.

* Reconstruction rule
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-RECONSTRUCTION
:END:

Logical reconstruction is a join by admitted fact identity.  It is not a join
of arbitrary endpoints that happen to co-occur in different posting lists.

Reconstruction validates:

- relation schema and arity;
- role assignment;
- all required qualifiers;
- provider and snapshot compatibility;
- provenance;
- truncation and completeness state.

Failure produces an incompatible or incomplete fact result, never a weakened
fact with erased fields.

* Forward and inverse lookup
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-DIRECTIONS
:END:

A provider capability descriptor states which argument roles support direct
lookup.  The planner may bind any declared role; it does not assume a universal
subject/object orientation.

When an inverse index is absent, the provider returns a capability gap or a
bounded alternate action.  SearchLoop does not emulate completeness through
unbounded scans.

* Query-pack contract
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-QUERY-PACK
:END:

A posting query pack contains:

- clause and obligation identity;
- relation kind and schema;
- bound role/value pairs;
- unresolved variable domains;
- qualifier predicates;
- canonical workspace and source generation;
- provider route and artifact identity;
- scope, completeness, and freshness requirements;
- result and byte budgets;
- projection class;
- continuation and searched-action identity.

Equivalent rendered CLI strings do not define query-pack equality.

* Result-pack contract
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-RESULT-PACK
:END:

A result pack reports:

- query-pack digest;
- candidate fact identifiers;
- selected logical facts;
- new bindings;
- truncation and complete-scope status;
- provider coverage;
- freshness witnesses;
- actual owner, selector, provider, byte, and latency costs;
- cache state;
- contradictions;
- graph-escalation proposals or mandatory reasons.

A no-hit result names whether lookup coverage was complete.  No-hit without
complete admitted scope is not negative evidence.

* Admission before use
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-ADMISSION
:END:

Posting results enter the SearchLoop evidence set only after:

- provider identity and capability validation;
- source generation validation;
- logical fact reconstruction;
- qualifier preservation;
- provenance admission;
- policy-overlay checks;
- contradiction detection.

Rank and utility apply after admission.  They cannot admit an invalid fact.

* Graph escalation classes
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-ESCALATION-CLASSES
:END:

** Proof-mandatory escalation

Always escalate for:

- enumeration and absence;
- complete dependency or reachability closure;
- contradiction reconciliation;
- cross-provider or cross-generation joins;
- alias, relocation, or owner ambiguity;
- policy findings that require global scope;
- exact selector reachability that cannot be justified locally.

** Structural-complexity escalation

Escalate when:

- unresolved bindings exceed the admitted expansion bound;
- hop depth exceeds the relation-class limit;
- relation degree causes candidate explosion;
- clause dependencies form an SCC without decreasing measure;
- required joins lack a complete direct index.

** Cost-recommended escalation

The cost model MAY recommend graph projection when predicted clause work
exceeds bounded graph work.  This recommendation is advisory and cannot
override proof-mandatory escalation.

* Escalation predicate inputs
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-ESCALATION-PREDICATE
:END:

The predicate consumes admitted facts and measured state:

- obligation class;
- clause count and state distribution;
- unresolved-variable domains;
- candidate degree and hop estimates;
- namespace, provider, and snapshot boundaries;
- required completeness;
- contradictions;
- spent and remaining budget;
- actual historical costs under the current provider digest.

Provider self-reported cost alone cannot force or suppress escalation.

* Graph projection request
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-GRAPH-REQUEST
:END:

The clause frontier emits a typed request containing:

- unresolved obligations;
- admitted facts and provenance references;
- bound variables and canonical selectors;
- required join, path, completeness, or contradiction goals;
- workspace, snapshot, provider, schema, and policy identities;
- graph budget and projection class;
- continuation identity.

The graph layer may reuse admitted facts but revalidates their compatibility.

* Graph result handoff
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-GRAPH-HANDOFF
:END:

Graph projection returns:

- admitted graph generation;
- graph-route evidence;
- candidate discharge evidence and unresolved obligation identities;
- newly justified bindings;
- contradictions and scope proofs;
- exact selectors requiring materialization;
- truncation and budget state;
- closure-admission evidence.

The clause frontier does not translate graph rank directly into resolved state.
The closure validator applies the declared discharge rules.  The graph layer
does not own the final discharged-obligation set.

* Enumeration and absence protocol
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-CLOSED-WORLD
:END:

An enumeration or absence receipt MUST name:

- the finite admitted scope;
- all providers and relation classes responsible for that scope;
- provider coverage and source generation;
- non-truncation proof;
- graph or index completeness class;
- invalidation dependencies;
- the exact proposition established.

Changing scope, provider coverage, or generation invalidates the receipt.

* Provider asymmetry
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-PROVIDER-ASYMMETRY
:END:

Shared semantics do not require identical performance capabilities.  Providers
may differ in indexes, relation extensions, and cost.

They may not differ silently in the meaning of shared relation kinds,
completeness flags, selector authority, or typed failure.  Unsupported shared
semantics fail closed with actionable capability receipts.

* Candidate-pool accounting
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-ACCOUNTING
:END:

The planner records both estimated and actual:

- posting reads;
- logical facts reconstructed;
- unique owners and selectors;
- provider dispatches;
- graph nodes and relations projected;
- exact source materializations;
- candidate, evidence, and model-facing bytes;
- closure work caused by each candidate.

Policies are evaluated against actual downstream closure cost to resist
many-small-query and easy-clause gaming.

* Shadow migration receipts
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-SHADOW
:END:

During migration, clause-first and graph-first paths run against the same
obligation set.  The receipt compares:

- fact-set and selector-set differences;
- discharged-obligation differences;
- graph-escalation decision;
- closure outcome;
- missing qualifiers or provenance;
- latency, bytes, and provider calls;
- exact-source contradictions.

Scenario classes move from shadow to active only after unexplained semantic
differences are zero.

* Storage non-goals
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-RPGE-NON-GOALS
:END:

- storing raw source as posting payload;
- flattening n-ary facts into lossy binary edges;
- inferring closed-world coverage from index size;
- requiring one physical index layout across providers;
- allowing Turso or another database to own semantic truth;
- making a performance threshold a closure rule.
