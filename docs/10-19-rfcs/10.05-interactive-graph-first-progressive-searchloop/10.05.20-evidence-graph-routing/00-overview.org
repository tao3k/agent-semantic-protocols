#+title: Evidence graph and routing
#+property: rfc_layer evidence-graph-routing

* Ownership

This layer owns evidence-graph topology, relation postings, graph-router
escalation, bounded path selection, and the conditions under which a loop may
pay for additional graph expansion.

It does not own presentation syntax, cache invalidation, or Lean audit
mechanics.
