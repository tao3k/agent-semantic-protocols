#+title: SearchRoute Multi-Authority Fault Aggregation
#+subtitle: Conservative authority coverage and safe progressive-loop termination
#+options: toc:3 num:t

* Status

This proof-backed RFC follows RFC 10.05.93 and is the first new proof document
placed directly under the canonical Interactive Graph-First Progressive
SearchLoop tree.

It specifies protocol semantics, Lean proofs, and Org-Babel Typst mathematics.
Rust and Python implementation remain out of scope.

* Problem

RFC 10.05.93 validates one fault-bound snapshot against authority, membership,
epoch, time, and attestation policy.

A multi-authority deployment has a different problem. Several authorized
authorities can issue authentic reports for the same membership epoch, while
the resilience policy guarantees only that a required candidate set contains
at least one conservative authority.

Authenticity alone is not soundness. A Byzantine authority can authentically
report a bound below actual faulty weight.

The interactive SearchLoop also sees candidates progressively. A low partial
aggregate can pass the quorum threshold before a later required candidate
raises the conservative aggregate and makes the threshold fail.

* Normative clauses

** MAFA-AUTHENTIC: authenticity is not conservatism

An authentic authority report proves origin and integrity. It MUST NOT by
itself establish that actual faulty weight is at most the reported bound.

The graph MUST represent authenticity and conservative-witness evidence as
different predicates.

** MAFA-COVERAGE: complete candidate coverage

The active multi-authority policy MUST define the required candidate coverage
for one membership epoch.

Before aggregation is final, the SearchLoop MUST establish that:

- all policy-required candidate positions are resolved; and
- the included candidate set contains at least one conservative witness under
  the policy's resilience assumption.

The loop MUST NOT infer coverage merely from the number of received reports.

** MAFA-WITNESS: conservative witness

A conservative witness is an included candidate whose declared bound is at
least actual faulty voting weight.

The witness need not be identified to the runtime if the authority resilience
proof establishes that at least one included candidate is conservative.

** MAFA-MAX: safe maximum aggregation

When candidate coverage guarantees at least one conservative witness, the
aggregate bound MUST be at least every included candidate bound. Concrete
maximum aggregation satisfies this requirement.

It follows that actual faulty weight is at most the aggregate maximum.

If every individual admitted authority is independently proven sound, smaller
aggregates may also be safe. This RFC addresses the weaker Byzantine-authority
assumption where only one included witness is guaranteed.

** MAFA-MONOTONE: progressive aggregation

Adding a candidate MUST NOT decrease maximum aggregation.

This monotonicity means an early low aggregate is not a stable final verdict.
The threshold can move only toward failure as required higher candidates are
added.

** MAFA-LOOP: safe termination

The interactive loop may finalize the fault aggregate only after:

#+begin_example
required authority coverage complete
  + conservative-witness guarantee established
  + maximum computed over the complete required candidate set
  -> final aggregate receipt
#+end_example

Arithmetic threshold success on a partial candidate set is not a stopping
condition.

** MAFA-REJECT: adversarial counterexamples

The proof surface MUST retain both counterexamples:

- minimum aggregation can select a Byzantine low report below actual faulty
  weight; and
- a partial low aggregate can pass the threshold before the expanded aggregate
  fails it.

** MAFA-CACHE: aggregate receipt identity

The aggregate receipt MUST bind:

- authority-policy identity;
- membership and epoch identity;
- complete required candidate-set identity;
- aggregation algorithm version; and
- ordered or canonicalized input snapshot identities.

Changing coverage or any required input invalidates the aggregate receipt.

* Interactive and graph consequence

The graph router must expose authority aggregation as a frontier, not one
opaque query:

#+begin_example
discover required authority positions
  -> validate candidate snapshots independently
  -> prove policy coverage
  -> derive conservative-witness guarantee
  -> compute final maximum
  -> evaluate quorum-intersection threshold
#+end_example

Each step produces a compact receipt and may terminate early on a failed
predicate. Successful arithmetic before coverage completion remains
provisional and MUST NOT be cached as a final verdict.

This is progressive-first search with a proof-aware stopping rule: the loop
returns the minimum evidence needed for the current step, but it does not omit
an unresolved coverage obligation merely to reduce interaction rounds.

* Safety and liveness boundary

Maximum aggregation is conservative under the one-honest-witness assumption,
but a Byzantine authority can report an arbitrarily high bound and prevent the
quorum threshold from passing.

This is a liveness failure, not a safety failure.

* Proof-discovered next boundary

The next proof boundary must model a concrete authority-set resilience policy:

- authority quorum or weighted authority threshold;
- maximum Byzantine authorities;
- order-statistic aggregation;
- equivocation;
- authority-set rotation; and
- the conditions under which an aggregate is both safe and live.

It must also prove that the progressive SearchLoop has enough coverage evidence
to apply that policy before declaring the aggregation frontier complete.
