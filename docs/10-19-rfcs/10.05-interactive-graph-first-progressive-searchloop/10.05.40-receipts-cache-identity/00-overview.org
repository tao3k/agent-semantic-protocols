* Ownership

This layer owns evidence receipts, artifact ledgers, semantic cache keys,
invalidation, model-cache identity, calibration history, and recovery state.
Search-result reuse and model prefix-cache reuse are separate mechanisms and
must remain separately observable.

It does not own SearchLoop transition order or graph traversal policy.
