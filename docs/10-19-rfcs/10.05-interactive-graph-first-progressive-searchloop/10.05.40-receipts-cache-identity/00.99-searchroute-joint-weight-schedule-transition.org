#+title: RFC 10.05.40.00.99 - Joint Weight-Schedule Transition
#+startup: overview

* Status

Draft, proof-backed.

This module governs authority-set rotation for weighted authority aggregation.
It refines RFCs 00.97 and 00.98. It changes the receipt, cache, and SearchLoop
transition contract only. It does not authorize Rust or Python implementation.

* Abstract

An authority report has no schedule-independent voting weight. During
authority-set rotation, the same report evidence may refer to an authority
present in both the old and new snapshots, but its resolved weight can differ.

The transition has three monotone phases:

#+name: searchroute-joint-weight-schedule-transition-model
#+begin_src typst
#let old-only = 0
#let joint = 1
#let new-only = 2
#let valid-transition(from, to) = from <= to
#assert(valid-transition(old-only, joint))
#assert(valid-transition(joint, new-only))
#assert(valid-transition(old-only, new-only))
#assert(not valid-transition(new-only, old-only))
"ok"
#+end_src

The joint phase authorizes two schedule-specific aggregation lanes. It does not
authorize one mixed-weight aggregation.

* Transition phases

** Old-only

Only the old authority-set snapshot and old weight schedule are authorized.
Reports resolved under the new schedule MUST be rejected with
=unauthorized-schedule-for-phase=.

** Joint

Both old and new schedules are authorized, but they remain separate proof
domains:

#+begin_example
old report materialization
  -> old admitted set
  -> old total weight
  -> old cut
  -> old weighted receipt

new report materialization
  -> new admitted set
  -> new total weight
  -> new cut
  -> new weighted receipt
#+end_example

A joint transition receipt MAY reference both weighted receipts. It MUST NOT
sum old and new weights into one total or use an old weight inside the new
ledger.

** New-only

Only the new authority-set snapshot and new weight schedule are authorized.
An old receipt can remain historical evidence, but cannot authorize a current
aggregation or cache hit.

** Monotonicity

Phase transitions MUST be monotone:

\[
\mathsf{oldOnly} \preceq \mathsf{joint}
  \preceq \mathsf{newOnly}.
\]

A transition from =newOnly= to =oldOnly= requires a new transition identity
and policy epoch. It MUST NOT be represented as regression within the same
transition.

* Schedule-bound report materialization

The signed report evidence and its weighted materialization are different
objects.

The evidence identity binds what the authority reported:

#+begin_example
reportEvidenceId = H(
  canonicalAuthorityId,
  reportValue,
  reportEpoch,
  signatureEvidence
)
#+end_example

The materialization identity additionally binds how that evidence is interpreted:

#+begin_example
weightedReportMaterializationId = H(
  reportEvidenceId,
  authoritySetSnapshotId,
  weightScheduleDigest,
  resolvedVotingWeight,
  aggregationPolicyVersion
)
#+end_example

The same =reportEvidenceId= MAY appear in both joint lanes when authorized.
The =weightedReportMaterializationId= MUST differ whenever the snapshot,
schedule, resolved weight, or policy differs.

One materialized report cannot simultaneously realize two different weights.
Reusing its old serialized weight under a new schedule is a typed identity
error, not a cache optimization.

* Weighted receipt compatibility

A weighted receipt key contains:

- =authoritySetSnapshotId=;
- =weightScheduleDigest=;
- =epoch=;
- =admittedReportSetDigest=;
- =totalCollectedWeight=;
- =faultWeightBudget=;
- =cutWeight=;
- =selectedValue=.

Compatibility requires equality of every field. In particular:

#+name: searchroute-joint-weight-schedule-transition-proposition
#+begin_src typst
#let receipt-separation = $
  S_"old" != S_"new"
  or D_"old" != D_"new"
  arrow.r.double
  R_"old" != R_"new"
$
#let same-value-not-enough = $
  V_"old" = V_"new"
  and D_"old" != D_"new"
  arrow.r.double
  R_"old" != R_"new"
$
#let joint-ledgers = $
  W_"old" = sum_i w_i^"old"
  and W_"new" = sum_i w_i^"new"
$
#assert(type(receipt-separation) == content)
#assert(type(same-value-not-enough) == content)
#assert(type(joint-ledgers) == content)
"ok"
#+end_src

Here \(S\) is the authority-set snapshot identity, \(D\) is the weight-schedule
digest, \(V\) is the selected bound value, and \(R\) is the weighted receipt
identity.

Equal selected values do not make old and new receipts compatible.

* Proof-discovered gaps

** Authorization is not compatibility

During =joint=, both schedules are authorized. This means either schedule may
produce a receipt. It does not mean:

- their weights may be mixed;
- one receipt may satisfy the other's quorum;
- one cache entry may be reused for the other;
- equal selected values collapse them into one identity.

The protocol MUST represent authorization and compatibility as distinct
predicates.

** A joint total is invalid

The expression:

\[
W_{\mathrm{joint}} =
  W_{\mathrm{old}} + W_{\mathrm{new}}
\]

usually double-counts shared authorities and combines weights from different
semantic domains. It MUST NOT be used for quorum, cut selection, or fault
budget comparison.

A joint composite contains two totals; it does not invent a third total.

** Shared report evidence is not shared materialization

If authority \(a\) has weight three in the old schedule and weight five in the
new schedule, one materialized =votingWeight= field cannot prove both. The
evidence payload may be shared by digest, but each lane must independently
resolve and commit the schedule-owned weight.

** Phase alone is not sufficient cache identity

Two transitions can both be in =joint= while using different old/new snapshot
pairs. The transition identity and both snapshot identities must be bound.

* Normative joint transition receipt

A joint transition receipt MUST include:

- =transitionId=;
- =transitionPolicyVersion=;
- =transitionPhase = "joint"=;
- =oldAuthoritySetSnapshotId=;
- =newAuthoritySetSnapshotId=;
- =oldWeightScheduleDigest=;
- =newWeightScheduleDigest=;
- =oldWeightedReceiptId=;
- =newWeightedReceiptId=;
- =oldReceiptVerificationStatus=;
- =newReceiptVerificationStatus=;
- =jointDecisionPolicyVersion=;
- =issuedAt= and =expiresAt=.

The two weighted receipts MUST remain independently retrievable and
independently auditable.

The joint receipt MUST NOT copy a single =totalCollectedWeight=,
=faultWeightBudget=, or =cutWeight= into a field that appears to govern both
schedules.

If both lanes are required by policy, absence or failure of either lane MUST
produce =incomplete-joint-receipt-pair=. If policy permits one lane to remain
advisory, that asymmetry MUST be explicit in =jointDecisionPolicyVersion=.

* Receipt and cache identity

The joint composite identity MUST bind:

#+begin_example
jointTransitionReceiptId = H(
  transitionId,
  transitionPolicyVersion,
  transitionPhase,
  oldAuthoritySetSnapshotId,
  newAuthoritySetSnapshotId,
  oldWeightScheduleDigest,
  newWeightScheduleDigest,
  oldWeightedReceiptId,
  newWeightedReceiptId,
  jointDecisionPolicyVersion,
  issuedAt,
  expiresAt
)
#+end_example

Cache rules:

- an old-only receipt cannot satisfy a new-only lookup;
- an old weighted receipt cannot satisfy the new lane of a joint lookup;
- a joint composite hit requires exact old and new receipt identities;
- advancing phase invalidates phase-authorizing cache entries;
- equal =selectedValue= does not relax any identity comparison.

Search-result cache validity remains distinct from model prefix-cache reuse.
The compact rendering of two receipts may obtain a model cache hit, but the
runtime must still verify phase, transition identity, schedule, and expiry.

* SearchLoop and graph-router consequences

The progressive graph path is:

#+begin_example
resolve transition identity and phase
  -> select authorized schedule lane
  -> materialize schedule-owned report weights
  -> verify lane-specific weighted receipt
  -> during joint, verify the other required lane
  -> emit compact joint composite
  -> apply joint decision policy
  -> route
#+end_example

The compact joint projection SHOULD expose:

- transition and phase;
- old/new snapshot and schedule identities;
- old/new selected values and margins;
- old/new receipt verification states;
- the joint decision result;
- inspect transitions for either lane.

=inspect old-lane= and =inspect new-lane= progressively open the corresponding
00.98 concrete cut certificate. The default route does not dump both report
lists, preserving token efficiency and model prefix stability.

No complex transition-specific CLI family is required. These are typed
interactive transitions that a CLI adapter may render.

* Failure receipts

The protocol adds:

- =phase-regression=;
- =unauthorized-schedule-for-phase=;
- =mixed-weight-schedule=;
- =stale-transition-identity=;
- =weight-materialization-snapshot-mismatch=;
- =incomplete-joint-receipt-pair=;
- =joint-receipt-policy-mismatch=.

Every failure receipt MUST bind =transitionId=, phase, expected schedule
identity, observed schedule identity, and a typed continuation or terminal
reason.

* Lean realization

The proof owner is:

#+begin_example
packages/proofs/ASPProof/
  SearchRouteAdmissionRetryCacheRejoinJointWeightScheduleTransition.lean
#+end_example

It proves:

- old-only to joint to new-only is a valid monotone progression;
- new-only cannot regress to old-only within one transition;
- old-only rejects the new schedule;
- new-only rejects the old schedule;
- joint authorizes both schedules as separate lanes;
- receipt compatibility binds the weight-schedule digest;
- changing schedules invalidates receipt compatibility;
- equal selected values do not imply compatible receipts;
- a joint pair remains two receipts even when selected values match;
- one materialized weight cannot realize two different schedule weights.

* Non-goals

This module does not yet prove:

- how two valid lane results are fused into one routing bound;
- safety or liveness when old and new selected values differ;
- transition completion conditions;
- garbage collection of expired old receipts;
- recovery after a crash between old and new lane publication;
- fractional voting-weight schedules.

* Next proof boundary

The next module should prove the joint decision policy. If both old and new
receipts independently certify values in the honest interval \([A,L]\), a
deterministic fusion such as their maximum remains in that interval:

\[
A \le \max(S_{\mathrm{old}}, S_{\mathrm{new}}) \le L.
\]

The proof must preserve both input receipt identities and produce a new joint
decision receipt. It must not overwrite either input receipt or pretend the
maximum was selected by one weight schedule.
