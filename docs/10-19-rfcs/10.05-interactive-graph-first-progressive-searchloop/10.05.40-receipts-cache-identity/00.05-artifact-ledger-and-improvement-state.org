#+TITLE: Artifact Ledger and Improvement State
#+OPTIONS: toc:2

#+property: rfc_layer 10.05.40-receipts-cache-identity

* Decision

Self-improving SearchLoop separates a lossless, append-only artifact ledger from
a replaceable derived improvement state.

The ledger preserves what happened.  Improvement state proposes how future
plans should be selected.  Neither changes RFC semantics, shared schemas,
closure rules, provider authority, or Lean theorem statements automatically.

* Artifact ledger

The ledger records content-addressed events such as:

- intent, anchors, workspace, and source generation;
- obligation graph and selected evidence plan;
- branch actions and provider receipts;
- evidence, contradictions, and freshness checks;
- cache decisions;
- closure-validator result;
- user correction or downstream task outcome;
- cost, latency, packet bytes, and model-facing bytes;
- Lean counterexample or failed proof identity;
- promotion, rejection, rollback, and policy-version events.

Events are immutable.  Corrections append new events and relations rather than
rewriting history.

* Improvement state

Improvement state is a reproducible projection of an explicit ledger prefix.
It may contain:

- branch-selection priors;
- cost and latency estimates;
- known hard-negative patterns;
- cache admission and prewarm hints;
- provider reliability observations;
- counterexample-triggering scenario classes;
- candidate planning policies;
- evaluation and promotion status.

It is disposable: deleting and rebuilding it from the same ledger and policy
version MUST produce the same semantic state.

* Fixed semantic kernel

Automated improvement cannot modify:

- RFC normative clauses;
- obligation discharge or sufficiency rules;
- closure and fail-closed invariants;
- shared packet schemas;
- workspace, snapshot, provider, or selector authority;
- Lean theorem definitions or accepted assumptions;
- evidence provenance requirements.

Changing any of these requires an RFC and proof-cycle revision, not experience
promotion.

* Promotion pipeline

An experience-derived proposal moves through:

1. =candidate=: derived from ledger evidence with named scope;
2. =replay=: evaluated against the originating and held-out episodes;
3. =adversarial=: tested against known counterexamples and hard negatives;
4. =shadow=: produces decisions without controlling execution;
5. =bounded-active=: controls only declared advisory choices;
6. =accepted= or =rejected=: recorded with evidence and policy digest;
7. =rollback=: restores the prior accepted projection without changing ledger
   history.

Normative proposals stop before =bounded-active= and enter the RFC → AXLE →
Lean repair loop.

* Improvement safety

An accepted advisory policy MUST be a refinement of the SearchLoop transition
system:

- it may choose among already allowed plans;
- it may reduce cost or latency;
- it may improve branch ordering or cache admission;
- it cannot add an otherwise forbidden transition;
- it cannot suppress a required obligation;
- it cannot declare a new terminal state;
- it cannot reinterpret a provider failure as evidence.

Closure remains independently validated.

* Long-horizon operation

Long-running sessions checkpoint compact continuation and improvement-state
identity, not a rewritten summary treated as ground truth.  Raw evidence remains
addressable through ledger artifacts.

Compaction records which facts were retained, omitted, superseded, or
invalidated.  A later loop can request exact evidence without replaying the
entire conversational history into model context.

* Reward and evaluation boundary

Self-reported model confidence is not a reward.  Evaluation uses external or
independently checkable outcomes:

- closure-validator status;
- witness freshness;
- downstream task and test receipts;
- user correction;
- Lean proof or counterexample status;
- cost and latency under equal evidence quality;
- regression and held-out scenario results.

Optimizing command count or token use while weakening evidence is a failed
proposal.

* Feedback into RFC and Lean

Ledger evidence may reveal:

- repeated =no-sound-plan= states;
- missing obligation kinds;
- invalid sufficiency alternatives;
- unstable cache dependencies;
- non-progress loops;
- unsafe policy refinements;
- projection facts unavailable to the agent.

These observations create RFC defect candidates.  Accepted normative repairs
change the RFC digest, regenerate the AXLE declaration bundle, invalidate
dependent Lean receipts, and replay the affected proof set.

* Acceptance receipts

Improvement receipts report:

- ledger prefix and policy digest;
- training, replay, held-out, and adversarial episode identities;
- proposal scope;
- quality, cost, latency, and cache deltas;
- closure regressions and counterexamples;
- shadow and bounded-active duration;
- acceptance, rejection, or rollback reason;
- deterministic rebuild digest.

No promotion is accepted from aggregate success rate alone.
