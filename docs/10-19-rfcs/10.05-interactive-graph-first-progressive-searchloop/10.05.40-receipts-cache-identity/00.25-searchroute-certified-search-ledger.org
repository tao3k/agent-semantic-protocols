* Lean-discovered correction to RFC 10.05.24

RFC 10.05.24 introduced =RealizedTraceCatalogComplete=.  Its exact quantified
obligation is represented by =UniverseComplete= in the embedded Org-Babel
Typst model below: every realized and feasible candidate belongs to the
projected catalog.

Every feasible realized candidate therefore had to be the projection of a
materialized closed costed trace.

That condition is sufficient for global optimality, but it is stronger than a
branch-and-bound SearchLoop needs.  If a certified lower bound proves that a
frontier cannot beat the selected candidate, expanding every candidate in that
frontier into a full inspect trace wastes:

- semantic-search evidence tokens;
- prompt tokens;
- model/tool interaction rounds;
- SearchLoop transitions;
- trace storage and audit payload.

Lean exposed a second problem: =ScheduleComplete= only states that the
scheduler's =inspect= queue is empty.  It does not prove that all feasible
realized candidates belong to any candidate universe.  Empty scheduler state
and semantic universe completeness are independent obligations.

The corrected global result therefore uses a certified search ledger instead
of demanding a fully materialized realized trace catalog.

* Snapshot identity
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-SNAPSHOT
:END:

A =RankedDAGSnapshot= pairs:

- a snapshot digest;
- the ranked DAG whose routes are being considered.

The certified ledger MUST bind its =CandidateUniverse.snapshotDigest= to this
snapshot digest.  Its =CoverageReceipt= MUST bind its claimed snapshot digest
to the same candidate universe.

The exact equality chain is =SnapshotBound= in the embedded Org-Babel Typst
model: the coverage, universe, and graph-snapshot digests are equal.

A coverage or frontier certificate from another snapshot MUST NOT be reused.
The graph and digest are one typed proof input, rather than unrelated receipt
fields which happen to carry equal strings.

* Visible realized catalog
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-VISIBLE
:END:

Only visible candidates require full trace provenance.

Every visible catalog entry contains:

- =TraceRouteContext=;
- =CostedClosedTraceReceipt=;
- its exact =GraphCandidate= projection;
- a =RankedDAG.Walk= realization witness.

Every projected visible candidate MUST also satisfy =graphFeasibleB= for the
active requirement and budget.

The visible selector MUST prove both membership of the selected candidate and
lexicographic dominance over every visible candidate.  The exact formula is
=VisibleOptimal= in the embedded Org-Babel Typst model.  Its four-axis cost
vector is defined once as =Cost= in the same block.

The selected entry therefore retains semantic-search cache provenance, model
prefix cache provenance, trace closure, graph realization, and exact cost.

* Candidate-universe completeness
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-UNIVERSE
:END:

The candidate universe MUST be complete for the active:

- ranked DAG snapshot;
- source and target;
- route requirement;
- graph and route budget.

The formal quantified obligation is =UniverseComplete= in the embedded
Org-Babel Typst model.

This is =CandidateComplete= over the candidate universe, not over the visible
trace catalog.

The distinction is essential:

- =U= contains compact candidate values required for the completeness proof;
- =V= contains visible candidates with full costed trace provenance;
- deferred frontiers cover the candidates in =U= which are not visible in =V=
  without requiring full trace
  materialization.

The current mathematical model still represents =U= as a finite list of
=GraphCandidate= values.  It removes full trace materialization for pruned
candidates, but it does not yet prove a symbolic or Merkle-addressed universe
whose candidate values are generated only on demand.  That is a later proof
boundary.

* Coverage ledger
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-COVERAGE
:END:

The ledger contains:

- candidate universe =U=;
- visible realized catalog =E=, whose projection is =V=;
- deferred certified partial frontiers =F=;
- a snapshot-bound =CoverageReceipt=;
- an equality between receipt frontiers and scheduler frontiers.

The exact coverage conservation formula is =Coverage= in the embedded
Org-Babel Typst model.

Each frontier carries a lower bound which is valid for every candidate it
covers.  Coverage is not a ranking claim; it is the conservation law ensuring
that no universe member disappears between visible and deferred state.

The receipt MUST keep visible and deferred ownership disjoint at the protocol
level even though the current theorem does not require list disjointness.
Duplicate coverage is safe for optimality but increases receipt size and should
be diagnosed as an efficiency defect.

* Scheduler closure
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-CLOSURE
:END:

=ScheduleComplete selected frontiers= means the scheduler has no frontier left
in the =inspect= partition.

For every deferred frontier, scheduler closure proves that the selected cost
is no worse than the frontier lower bound, while bound validity proves that
the lower bound is no worse than every candidate covered by that frontier.
The exact statements are =ScheduleClosed= and =FrontierBoundValid= in the
embedded Org-Babel Typst model.

Closure means that all deferred frontiers are safely prunable.  It does not
mean that the universe is complete, that a selected trace exists, or that the
snapshot identities match.  Those remain independent ledger fields.

* Certified global driver theorem
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-GLOBAL
:END:

Given a certified trace ledger with:

1. snapshot binding;
2. complete candidate universe;
3. feasible visible realized catalog;
4. successful visible selector;
5. universe coverage by visible candidates or deferred frontiers;
6. valid frontier lower bounds;
7. scheduler closure;

the selected candidate has visible trace provenance, realizes the requested
DAG route, and is no worse than every feasible realized candidate.  The exact
quantified theorem and its premises are defined once as =GlobalDriver= in the
embedded Org-Babel Typst model.

The proof route is candidate-universe completeness, followed by coverage
conservation, followed by either visible optimality or safe frontier pruning.

This theorem preserves RFC 10.05.24's global result without requiring every
pruned candidate to carry a materialized =CostedClosedTraceReceipt=.

* Non-implication counterexample
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCL-NONIMPLICATION
:END:

An empty frontier list satisfies scheduler closure.

Yet an independently incomplete candidate list can omit a feasible realized
route.  Lean constructs this counterexample using the one-node graph and the
existing graph-only route catalog.

The non-implication is represented exactly by =NonImplication= in the embedded
Org-Babel Typst model: =ScheduleComplete= does not imply
=CandidateComplete=.

Any protocol which treats an empty inspect queue as proof of semantic search
completeness is unsound.

* SearchLoop wire consequences

The agent-facing loop should expose one compact ledger state rather than a
large set of graph-search commands.

A closed response should contain or address:

- graph snapshot identity;
- selected visible trace identity;
- selected cost vector;
- visible selector receipt;
- candidate-universe completeness identity;
- coverage receipt identity;
- deferred frontier summary;
- scheduler closure;
- semantic-search cache provenance;
- model prefix cache provenance.

Large trace bodies, candidate lists, and frontier memberships SHOULD be
content-addressed and progressively inspectable.  The default response should
provide digests, counts, cost bounds, and the next meaningful inspect action.

The protocol should report at least:

- visible trace count;
- universe candidate count;
- deferred frontier count;
- pruned candidate count;
- bytes/tokens of materialized visible traces;
- bytes/tokens avoided by deferred trace materialization;
- SearchLoop rounds and transitions;
- semantic-search cache hits;
- model prefix cache hits.

* Mathematical model

The mathematical projection is embedded in this Org document.  =orgize eval
plan= extracts the named block and =typst eval --in -= parses it through stdin.

#+name: searchroute-certified-search-ledger-model
#+begin_src typst
#let Cost = $ K(c) =
  (h(c), u(c), r(c), t(c)) $

#let RankedDAGSnapshot = (
  digest: "SnapshotDigest",
  graph: "RankedDAG",
)

#let VisibleEntry = (
  trace_context: "TraceRouteContext",
  receipt: "CostedClosedTraceReceipt",
  candidate: "GraphCandidate",
  realization: "Walk(graph, source, target, graph_hops(candidate))",
)

#let SnapshotBound = $ d_"coverage" = d_"universe"
  = d_"graph snapshot" $

#let UniverseComplete = $ forall c,
  ("Realizes"(G, s, t, c) "and" "Feasible"(q, b, c))
    "implies" c in U $

#let Coverage = $ forall c in U,
  c in V "or"
  exists f in F, c in "candidates"(f) $

#let VisibleOptimal = $ cases(
  c^* in V,
  forall c in V, K(c^*) <=_"lex" K(c),
) $

#let FrontierBoundValid = $ forall f in F, forall c in "candidates"(f),
  "lower_bound"(f) <=_"lex" K(c) $

#let ScheduleClosed = $ forall f in F,
  K(c^*) <=_"lex" "lower_bound"(f) $

#let GlobalDriver = $ cases(
  SnapshotBound,
  UniverseComplete,
  Coverage,
  VisibleOptimal,
  FrontierBoundValid,
  ScheduleClosed,
) "implies" cases(
  exists e in E, pi(e) = c^*,
  "Realizes"(G, s, t, c^*),
  forall c,
    ("Realizes"(G, s, t, c) "and" "Feasible"(q, b, c))
      "implies" K(c^*) <=_"lex" K(c),
) $

#let NonImplication = $ "ScheduleComplete"(c^*, ())
  "does not imply" "CandidateComplete"(U) $

#let TraceMaterializationBoundary = $ cases(
  V = "map"(pi, E),
  "full_trace_required"(V),
  "frontier_certificate_only"(U without V),
) $

#let Main = (
  Cost,
  RankedDAGSnapshot,
  VisibleEntry,
  SnapshotBound,
  UniverseComplete,
  Coverage,
  VisibleOptimal,
  FrontierBoundValid,
  ScheduleClosed,
  GlobalDriver,
  NonImplication,
  TraceMaterializationBoundary,
)

"ok"
#+end_src

* Non-goals

This RFC does not:

- implement Rust provider behavior;
- require every pruned candidate to carry a full costed trace;
- claim scheduler closure implies candidate-universe completeness;
- prove a symbolic or Merkle candidate universe;
- make semantic-search cache and model prefix cache interchangeable;
- prescribe a complex public CLI command tree;
- require standalone Typst sources or PDFs.

* Remaining proof boundary

The next layer should replace the explicit finite =CandidateUniverse.candidates=
list with a content-addressed or symbolic universe whose completeness is
derived from constructive DAG and route enumeration.

That layer must prove that a compact universe commitment supports:

- membership or exclusion proofs for feasible realized candidates;
- frontier coverage without listing every candidate in the prompt;
- deterministic snapshot identity;
- incremental inspection;
- the same global driver theorem.
