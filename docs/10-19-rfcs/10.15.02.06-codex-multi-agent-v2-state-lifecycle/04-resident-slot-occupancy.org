#+TITLE: Per-slot resident occupancy

“Single resident” is never a workspace-wide or root-session-wide restriction.
It means only this invariant:

#+begin_quote
For each configured resident route key, at most one physical resident
generation occupies that route's slot.
#+end_quote

Different resident profiles may be live concurrently, and any number of
temporary agents may coexist with them.  Replacement changes one slot from
incumbent to vacant to replacement after the close/release receipts are indexed.
