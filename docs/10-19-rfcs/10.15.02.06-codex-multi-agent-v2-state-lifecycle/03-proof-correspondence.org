#+title: RFC 10.15.02.06.03 — LifeSession proof correspondence
#+options: toc:2

* Three-view contract

The proof specification is maintained in three synchronized views:

- Org states normative lifecycle and Rust migration clauses.
- Typst states the mathematical predicates inside Org-Babel blocks.
- Lean makes the reducer and non-implication claims executable.

Mermaid state diagrams are a fourth presentation view.  Every lifecycle edge
in a diagram must name both a state transition and its durable fact or rejection
condition.

* Lean modules

- =ASPAgentLifeSessionRefinement.lean=: name projection, binding authority,
  rebind progress, and one-command public surface;
- =ASPAgentLifeSessionClose.lean=: archive orthogonality, revision and
  generation fences, stable-key close, shutdown timeout, late termination,
  path release, and fresh-generation replacement; and
- =ASPAgentLifeSessionAggregate.lean=: cross-protocol authority separation and
  durable-dispatch revocation during close.

Their direct audits enumerate 47 theorem declarations.  All declarations must
remain axiom-free.  In particular, computational fence equality must reduce in
the kernel; the audit must not add =propext= merely to accommodate a convenient
boolean comparison implementation.

* AXLE obligations

AXLE MUST fail closed unless:

- all three Lean modules and their direct audits are present;
- every Lean owner has an Org reference to this module family;
- the Typst block evaluates through stdin;
- theorem-family identifiers cover binding, close, recovery, replacement, and
  aggregate authority separation;
- no declaration contains =sorryAx= or an allowed-axiom exception; and
- the receipt binds the exact Lean audit, Org clauses, and Typst source digest.

A successful =lake build= proves type correctness only.  AXLE owns independent
RFC correspondence and receipt completeness; executable Rust conformance
remains a later, separate gate.
