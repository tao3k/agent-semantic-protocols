#+title: SearchRoute Cache Rejoin Fault-Bound Authority
#+subtitle: Authorized, epoch-scoped, and temporally current safety bounds
#+options: toc:3 num:t

* Status

This proof-backed RFC follows RFC 10.05.92. It specifies fault-bound snapshot
admission and its composition with quorum-intersection safety. Rust and Python
implementation remain out of scope.

* Problem

RFC 10.05.92 binds an honest-overlap verdict to a fault-bound snapshot identity.
Identity prevents accidental reuse across different snapshots, but says nothing
about whether the selected snapshot is trustworthy.

A low numeric bound makes the honest-overlap inequality easier to satisfy. An
attacker can therefore present a snapshot that is:

- issued by an unauthorized authority;
- scoped to another membership or epoch;
- not backed by a valid attestation; or
- expired at the evaluation time.

Pure arithmetic still accepts some of these values. Snapshot admission must be
a separate proof boundary before the bound enters the intersection theorem.

* Normative clauses

** FBAS-SCOPE: membership binding

A fault-bound snapshot MUST contain the canonical membership digest to which
its claim applies. Snapshot admission MUST require equality with the current
membership digest.

** FBAS-EPOCH: epoch binding

A snapshot MUST bind an epoch identity. Admission MUST reject a snapshot from
another epoch even when the membership digest or numeric bound happens to
match.

** FBAS-AUTHORITY: authorization and attestation

A snapshot MUST identify its issuing authority and carry an attestation.

Admission requires both:

- the authority is authorized by the active fault-bound policy; and
- the attestation verifies for the complete snapshot content.

Authorization without verification and verification without authorization are
independently insufficient.

** FBAS-TIME: temporal validity

At evaluation time =t=, admission requires:

#+begin_example
issuedAt <= t < expiresAt
#+end_example

The expiration boundary is exclusive. A snapshot evaluated exactly at
=expiresAt= is stale.

** FBAS-SOUND: conservative authority contract

The authority verification contract MUST establish that actual faulty voting
weight is at most the declared bound.

This is the semantic assumption consumed by Lean. Merely verifying a signature
does not prove the bound is conservative unless the authority policy gives that
signature this meaning.

** FBAS-COMPOSE: honest-overlap conclusion

The router may conclude that actual faulty weight is below concrete
intersection weight only after combining:

- valid membership and epoch scope;
- authorized, verified, current fault-bound snapshot;
- authority soundness;
- two accepted quorum certificates;
- concrete pair accounting; and
- the fault-aware threshold from RFC 10.05.90.

** FBAS-REJECT: arithmetic is not admission

Passing the numeric threshold MUST NOT imply snapshot admission. Unauthorized
and stale low-bound counterexamples are part of the Lean proof surface.

** FBAS-CACHE: snapshot identity ownership

The honest-overlap verdict from RFC 10.05.92 MUST bind the content identity of
the validated snapshot, not only its numeric bound.

Snapshots carrying equal numeric bounds but different authority, epoch,
attestation, or validity interval are distinct evidence owners.

The focused proofs establish:

1. a valid snapshot conservatively bounds actual faulty weight;
2. validity reflects membership and epoch scope;
3. validity reflects the half-open time interval;
4. validated bounds compose with quorum intersection to prove actual honest
   overlap;
5. an unauthorized low bound can pass arithmetic but not admission; and
6. a stale low bound can pass arithmetic but not admission.

* Search efficiency consequence

Snapshot admission becomes a compact reusable graph node:

#+begin_example
snapshot content identity
  -> membership and epoch scope
  -> authority and attestation
  -> temporal validity
  -> conservative bound
#+end_example

The router can reject an unauthorized or stale snapshot before traversing
certificate and intersection subgraphs. A valid admission receipt lets
different pairwise accounting receipts reuse the same current snapshot proof.

Search-result caching may reuse that admission node only for the identical
snapshot content and validation-policy identity. Model prefix caching remains
independent and carries no evidence authority.

* Proof-discovered next boundary

One authorized authority with a sound verification contract is sufficient in
the current model. A deployed system may admit multiple authorities that issue
different current bounds for the same membership epoch.

The next proof boundary must define conservative multi-authority aggregation,
equivocation handling, policy identity, and authority-set rotation. Selecting
the minimum valid-looking bound is unsafe even when each individual signature
is authentic.
