#+title: RFC 10.05.82 — SearchRoute Cache Rejoin Policy Snapshot
#+startup: overview

* Status

Draft proof-bearing extension of RFC 10.05.81.

* Abstract

RFC 10.05.81 evaluates a sink receipt against one verifier-lifecycle snapshot.
That result can become stale before acknowledgement commits: admission state
may advance, or trust policy may rotate or revoke an epoch.

This RFC makes acknowledgement a proof-gated dual-snapshot commit.  A proposal
carries the admission revision, policy revision, and policy digest it observed.
Commit rechecks all three and reruns lifecycle admission against the current
bound policy snapshot.  The resulting acknowledgement records the exact
snapshots that authorized closure.

* Scope

The model treats admission state and policy snapshot as simultaneously visible
inputs to one commit predicate.  It does not yet prove atomic observation when
they live in separate storage or consensus domains.

* Normative clauses

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-POLICY — Policy snapshots are revisioned and content-bound

Each lifecycle snapshot MUST carry a monotone policy revision and a digest
committing to current epoch, previous epoch, overlap boundary, and revocation
state.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-OBSERVATION — Proposals carry both observed revisions

An acknowledgement proposal MUST carry its observed admission revision, policy
revision, and policy digest.  Ambient or most-recent values MUST NOT be silently
substituted.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-GATE — Commit revalidates both snapshots

Commit MUST require exact equality with the current admission revision, policy
revision, and policy digest, followed by RFC 10.05.81 lifecycle admission
against that current policy.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-STALE — Either changed snapshot invalidates validation

A proposal derived from an older admission revision or policy revision MUST
fail.  A proposal with a matching policy revision but wrong policy digest MUST
also fail.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-COMMIT — Closure records its authorization snapshots

The committed acknowledgement MUST retain the admission revision, policy
revision, policy digest, receipt, and closed publication obligation.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-DIGEST — Equal trusted digests identify equal policy content

The proof model requires an injective policy commitment.  Runtime deployments
MUST declare the concrete digest algorithm and collision policy; Lean does not
prove a cryptographic hash collision-free.

** ASP-RFC-10.05-CRPS-NONIMPLICATION — Dual comparison is not cross-store atomicity

Checking two revisions does not establish an atomic snapshot when the values
can change independently between checks and commit.

* Mathematical model

The executable model uses structural policy content as its idealized digest.

#+name: searchroute-cache-rejoin-policy-snapshot-model
#+begin_src typst
#let policy(content, revision) = (
  content: content,
  revision: revision,
  digest: content,
)
#let publication(admission-revision, phase) = (
  admission-revision: admission-revision,
  phase: phase,
)
#let proposal(
  expected-admission,
  expected-policy,
  expected-digest,
  lifecycle-admitted,
) = (
  expected-admission: expected-admission,
  expected-policy: expected-policy,
  expected-digest: expected-digest,
  lifecycle-admitted: lifecycle-admitted,
)
#let can-commit(current-policy, current-publication, candidate) = (
  candidate.expected-admission == current-publication.admission-revision
  and candidate.expected-policy == current-policy.revision
  and candidate.expected-digest == current-policy.digest
  and candidate.lifecycle-admitted
)
#let commit(current-policy, current-publication, candidate) = {
  assert(can-commit(current-policy, current-publication, candidate))
  (
    admission-revision: current-publication.admission-revision,
    policy-revision: current-policy.revision,
    policy-digest: current-policy.digest,
    phase: "acknowledged",
  )
}
#let current-policy = policy(
  (current: 5, previous: 4, overlap-end: 15, revoked-at: 12),
  20,
)
#let published = publication(8, "published")
#let valid = proposal(8, 20, current-policy.digest, true)
#let stale-admission = proposal(7, 20, current-policy.digest, true)
#let stale-policy = proposal(8, 19, current-policy.digest, true)
#let wrong-digest = proposal(8, 20, ("other-policy",), true)
#assert(can-commit(current-policy, published, valid))
#assert(not can-commit(current-policy, published, stale-admission))
#assert(not can-commit(current-policy, published, stale-policy))
#assert(not can-commit(current-policy, published, wrong-digest))
#let closed = commit(current-policy, published, valid)
#assert(closed.admission-revision == 8)
#assert(closed.policy-revision == 20)
#assert(closed.policy-digest == current-policy.digest)
#assert(closed.phase == "acknowledged")
#let rotated-policy = policy(
  (current: 6, previous: 5, overlap-end: 25, revoked-at: none),
  21,
)
#assert(not can-commit(rotated-policy, published, valid))
"ok"
#+end_src

The dual-snapshot gate is:

#let gate = $upright("CanCommitAck")(A, P, Q)
  arrow.r.double
  upright("expectedAdmission")(Q) = upright("revision")(A)
  and upright("expectedPolicy")(Q) = upright("revision")(P)
  and upright("expectedDigest")(Q) = upright("digest")(P)
  and upright("LifecycleAdmitted")(P, Q)$
#assert(type(gate) == content)
"ok"
#+end_src

* SearchLoop and graph-router consequence

The closed edge can expose one compact authorization tuple:

#+begin_example
(admissionRevision, policyRevision, policyDigest, receiptReference)
#+end_example

An audit expands this tuple only when policy freshness or revocation is in
question.

* Remaining proof boundary

If admission and policy use independent stores, one can change after its check
but before the other comparison commits.

A follow-up RFC must establish a joint linearization mechanism:

- one composite fencing token or transaction revision;
- atomic observation of admission and policy heads;
- one-winner acknowledgement commit;
- retry after either head changes;
- durable binding from the joint fence to the closed graph edge.
