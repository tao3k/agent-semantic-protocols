#+title: 10.05.10.00.72 Registry Snapshot Policy Resolution
#+startup: overview

* Status

Draft proof refinement for the policy-resolution trust chain.  No Rust or
Python runtime implementation is introduced.

* Problem

A content-addressed policy digest is meaningful only relative to the registry
snapshot that resolves it.  The same provider-local digest can resolve to
different semantics after a registry upgrade, migration, or corruption.

The trust chain is:

#+begin_quote
registry snapshot -> policy digest -> resolved semantics -> allocation receipt
#+end_quote

Comparing only the policy digest omits the first and decisive identity.

* Snapshot-bound resolution

A resolution certificate binds:

- registry snapshot digest;
- policy digest;
- resolved policy semantics;
- proof that the snapshot resolves that digest to those semantics.

Within one snapshot, one policy digest has at most one resolved semantics.
Replay comparability requires both snapshot digest and policy digest equality.

* Upgrade and revocation

Snapshot v1 may resolve policy digest 7 to =shared-only= while snapshot v2
resolves the same digest to =alpha-all=.  The digest equality does not authorize
replay across snapshots.

A revoked snapshot resolves no policy.  Old historical receipts remain
auditable, but no new allocation certificate may be issued from the revoked
resolution context.

* Cryptographic boundary

Lean models an injective finite snapshot digest.  Production digest
collision-resistance and byte-level snapshot identity remain owned by the
content-identity layer.  The protocol MUST provide the digest-resolution
receipt rather than treating a digest string as self-authenticating.

* Normative clauses

- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-SNAPSHOT=: snapshot digest uniquely identifies the resolved
  registry snapshot.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-UNIQUE=: policy resolution is functional within one
  snapshot.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-COMPARABLE=: equal snapshot and policy identities resolve
  equal semantics.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-POLICY-INSUFFICIENT=: policy digest equality alone does not
  imply semantic equality across snapshots.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-UPGRADE=: a snapshot upgrade rejects stale replay.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-REVOKED=: a revoked snapshot resolves no policy.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-NO-CERTIFICATE=: revoked policy resolution cannot produce a
  certificate.
- =ASP-RFC-10.05-RSR-REFLEXIVE=: a resolution certificate is replay comparable
  with itself.

* SearchLoop and Turbo Graph consequences

SearchLoop receipts SHOULD carry the registry-snapshot digest beside the policy
digest.  Python Turbo Graph should persist the snapshot, resolution, semantics,
and allocation as separate typed nodes and edges.

Registry upgrades create new projections.  They MUST NOT rewrite historical
allocations or silently reactivate revoked policy resolutions.
