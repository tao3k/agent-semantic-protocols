* Lean-discovered correction

RFC 10.05.25 still represented the complete candidate universe as a finite
=List GraphCandidate=.  Full costed traces were avoided for pruned candidates,
but every candidate value still existed in the proof object.

Replacing that list with only =rootDigest : Nat= is unsound.  A digest field
does not define:

- which candidates are members;
- how a membership proof is verified;
- how an exclusion proof is verified;
- whether membership and exclusion can both verify;
- whether a frontier commitment carries a valid lower bound;
- whether the commitment is complete for every feasible realized route.

Lean constructs two universes with the same digest and contradictory
membership predicates.  Therefore a digest-only universe has no binding
semantics.

The corrected protocol introduces a verifier-oriented commitment scheme.

* Commitment scheme
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-SCHEME
:END:

A =CandidateCommitmentScheme= defines:

- a membership-proof type;
- an exclusion-proof type;
- a valid-root predicate;
- the semantic membership relation for a root and candidate;
- a membership verifier;
- an exclusion verifier;
- membership soundness and completeness;
- exclusion soundness and completeness.

The scheme is abstract.  A provider may realize it through a Merkle tree,
authenticated database, polynomial commitment, deterministic finite
enumerator, or another content-addressed structure.

The shared protocol depends on verifier semantics, not on one hash algorithm
or storage engine.

Soundness is mandatory:

- a verified membership opening implies semantic membership;
- a verified exclusion opening implies semantic non-membership.

Completeness is also mandatory:

- every semantic member has an accepted membership proof;
- every semantic non-member under a valid root has an accepted exclusion
  proof.

These four obligations are defined exactly in =CandidateCommitmentScheme= in
the embedded Typst model.

Cryptographic collision resistance is not proved by Lean from =Nat=.  A real
provider MUST bind the abstract verifier to its concrete content-identity
scheme and algorithm identifier.

* Membership and exclusion openings
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-OPENING
:END:

A =MembershipOpening= contains:

- commitment root;
- candidate identity;
- provider-owned membership proof;
- successful verifier result.

An =ExclusionOpening= contains the analogous exclusion proof and verifier
result.

Soundness makes simultaneous verified membership and exclusion contradictory.
This is a theorem, not a procedural convention.

Every verified membership or exclusion opening MUST also establish that its
root is valid under the selected commitment scheme.  Exclusion completeness is
conditional on this valid-root witness.  An arbitrary digest which does not
commit to any candidate set has no meaningful non-membership proof.

Openings SHOULD be requested progressively.  The default SearchLoop packet
does not need every proof.  It may provide:

- root identity;
- visible candidate openings;
- frontier commitment identities;
- counts and bounds;
- an inspect action for one additional opening.

* Snapshot-bound committed universe
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-SNAPSHOT
:END:

A =CommittedCandidateUniverse= contains:

- graph snapshot digest;
- candidate-universe root digest.

The ledger binds the universe snapshot digest to the =RankedDAGSnapshot=
digest.  Coverage binds its claimed snapshot digest to the same universe.

Commitment completeness states that every candidate which realizes the
requested source-to-target route and satisfies the active requirement and
budget belongs to the committed universe.

The exact quantified condition is =CommitmentComplete= in the embedded Typst
model.

The completeness witness is independent from individual membership openings.
An opening answers whether one candidate belongs; completeness proves that no
feasible realized candidate exists outside the committed semantic universe.

* Symbolic frontier commitments
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-FRONTIER
:END:

A =CertifiedCommittedFrontier= contains:

- a frontier root digest;
- a graph/token/round/transition lower bound;
- a proof that the lower bound is valid for every semantic member of the
  frontier commitment.

It does not contain a list of candidate values.

If the selected candidate is no worse than the frontier lower bound, and the
frontier lower bound is no worse than a contained candidate, transitivity
proves that the selected candidate is no worse than that candidate.

This is =committed_frontier_pruning_is_safe= in Lean and
=CommittedFrontierSafe= in the Typst model.

* Committed coverage
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-COVERAGE
:END:

A =CommittedCoverageReceipt= conserves every semantic member of the candidate
universe.

For each contained candidate, the receipt proves one of:

- the candidate is visible and has a materialized realized trace entry;
- the candidate belongs to one of the deferred frontier commitments.

The exact obligation is =CommittedCoverage= in the embedded Typst model.

Coverage does not require all candidate values or openings in the default
packet.  Its proof object may be content-addressed and inspected on demand.

The provider MUST report enough commitment identity to prevent mixing:

- universe roots from another snapshot;
- frontier roots from another coverage receipt;
- openings verified under another commitment algorithm;
- lower bounds calculated from another cost order.

* Committed scheduler closure
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-CLOSURE
:END:

=CommittedScheduleComplete= proves that every deferred committed frontier is
prunable against the selected candidate.

This is stronger than an empty local queue and weaker than expanding every
frontier.  It is exactly the certificate needed by the global theorem:

- all visible candidates are handled by visible selector optimality;
- all deferred candidates are handled by committed frontier pruning.

The certificate MUST use the graph-hop, uncached-token, round, and transition
cost order defined by the preceding RFCs.

* Global driver theorem
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-GLOBAL
:END:

A =CommittedTraceLedger= combines:

1. commitment scheme;
2. snapshot-bound committed candidate universe;
3. complete universe witness;
4. visible feasible realized trace catalog;
5. committed frontier coverage;
6. successful visible selector;
7. committed scheduler closure.

The theorem proves:

- a visible trace entry projects to the selected candidate;
- the selected candidate realizes the requested DAG route;
- the selected candidate is no worse than every feasible realized candidate.

The exact theorem is =CommittedGlobalDriver= in the embedded Typst model and
=committed_trace_ledger_selects_global_driver= in Lean.

The proof no longer quantifies over a transmitted =List GraphCandidate=.
Instead it follows semantic membership through the universe and frontier
commitments.

* Digest-only counterexample
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ASP-RFC-10.05-SCU-DIGEST-ONLY
:END:

Lean constructs:

- a left digest-only universe containing every candidate;
- a right digest-only universe containing no candidate;
- equal root digests.

For any candidate, the two predicates disagree despite equal roots.

The exact counterexample is =DigestOnlyCounterexample= in the Typst model and
=digest_only_universe_is_not_binding= in Lean.

Therefore:

- a digest is an identifier, not a membership proof;
- root equality is not set equality without a verifier/binding contract;
- snapshot binding alone is not universe completeness;
- a cache hit for a root does not validate an opening under another scheme.

* Agent-facing projection

The public SearchLoop should remain small and interactive.

A compact closed packet should project:

- schema and commitment algorithm identifiers;
- graph snapshot digest;
- candidate-universe root;
- selected candidate and visible trace identity;
- selected cost vector;
- visible membership opening;
- coverage commitment;
- deferred frontier roots and lower bounds;
- scheduler closure receipt;
- semantic-search cache identity;
- model prefix cache identity;
- recommended next inspect action.

The agent can request one membership, exclusion, trace, or frontier opening
without constructing a large CLI expression.

The protocol should measure:

- packet tokens;
- number and bytes of membership openings;
- number and bytes of exclusion openings;
- visible trace tokens;
- candidate values avoided in the default packet;
- deferred trace bodies avoided;
- frontier count;
- interaction rounds and transitions;
- semantic-search and model-prefix cache hits separately.

#+begin_src typst
#let Cost = $ K(c) =
  (h(c), u(c), r(c), t(c)) $

#let CandidateCommitmentScheme = (
  membership_proof_type: "MembershipProof",
  exclusion_proof_type: "ExclusionProof",
  valid_root: "ValidRoot(root)",
  contains: "Contains(root, candidate)",
  verify_membership: "VerifyMember(root, candidate, proof)",
  verify_exclusion: "VerifyExcluded(root, candidate, proof)",
)

#let MembershipSound = $ "VerifyMember"(rho, c, p) = #true
  "implies" "Contains"(rho, c) $

#let MembershipComplete = $ "Contains"(rho, c)
  "implies" exists p, "VerifyMember"(rho, c, p) = #true $

#let ExclusionSound = $ "VerifyExcluded"(rho, c, x) = #true
  "implies" "not" "Contains"(rho, c) $

#let ExclusionComplete = $ "not" "Contains"(rho, c)
  "and" "ValidRoot"(rho)
  "implies" exists x, "VerifyExcluded"(rho, c, x) = #true $

#let OpeningRootValidity = $ cases(
  "VerifyMember"(rho, c, p) = #true,
  "VerifyExcluded"(rho, c, x) = #true,
) "implies" "ValidRoot"(rho) $

#let OpeningConsistency = $ cases(
  "VerifyMember"(rho, c, p) = #true,
  "VerifyExcluded"(rho, c, x) = #true,
) "implies" bot $

#let CommittedCandidateUniverse = (
  snapshot_digest: $d_"snapshot"$,
  root_digest: $rho_"universe"$,
)

#let CommitmentComplete = $ forall c,
  ("Realizes"(G, s, t, c) "and" "Feasible"(q, b, c))
    "implies" "Contains"(rho_"universe", c) $

#let CertifiedCommittedFrontier = (
  root_digest: $rho_f$,
  lower_bound: $L_f$,
  bound_valid: $ forall c,
    "Contains"(rho_f, c) "implies" L_f <=_"lex" K(c) $,
)

#let CommittedCoverage = $ forall c,
  "Contains"(rho_"universe", c) "implies" cases(
    c in V,
    exists f in F, "Contains"(rho_f, c),
  ) $

#let CommittedScheduleComplete = $ forall f in F,
  K(c^*) <=_"lex" L_f $

#let CommittedFrontierSafe = $ cases(
  "Contains"(rho_f, c),
  L_f <=_"lex" K(c),
  K(c^*) <=_"lex" L_f,
) "implies" K(c^*) <=_"lex" K(c) $

#let CommittedGlobalDriver = $ cases(
  "SnapshotBound",
  CommitmentComplete,
  CommittedCoverage,
  "VisibleOptimal",
  CommittedScheduleComplete,
) "implies" cases(
  exists e in E, pi(e) = c^*,
  "Realizes"(G, s, t, c^*),
  forall c,
    ("Realizes"(G, s, t, c) "and" "Feasible"(q, b, c))
      "implies" K(c^*) <=_"lex" K(c),
) $

#let DigestOnlyCounterexample = $ exists U_l, U_r, c, cases(
  "root"(U_l) = "root"(U_r),
  "Contains"(U_l, c),
  "not" "Contains"(U_r, c),
) $

* Non-goals

This RFC does not:

- select a concrete Merkle or database implementation;
- prove collision resistance of a concrete hash function;
- modify Rust provider code;
- transmit every candidate or opening in the default packet;
- merge semantic-search and model-prefix cache identity;
- add a complex public CLI command tree;
- use raw LaTeX or standalone Typst sources.

* Remaining proof boundary

The next layer should instantiate =CandidateCommitmentScheme= with a concrete
finite authenticated construction.

That construction must prove:

- deterministic canonical candidate encoding;
- domain-separated root calculation;
- membership and exclusion verifier soundness;
- snapshot and commitment algorithm binding;
- deterministic receipt serialization;
- compatibility with incremental SearchLoop inspection;
- measurable token savings against the explicit candidate-list ledger.
