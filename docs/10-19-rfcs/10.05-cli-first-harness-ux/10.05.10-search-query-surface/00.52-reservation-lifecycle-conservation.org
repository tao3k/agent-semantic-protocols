#+title: RFC 10.05.10.00.52 — Reservation Lifecycle Conservation
#+startup: overview

* Status

Draft research specification.

This RFC composes ordinary reservation, execution, quarantine, and authorized
resolution into one resource-conserving lifecycle. It does not add CLI commands
or authorize implementation.

* Problem

RFC 00.36 proves local reservation accounting. RFC 00.50 and RFC 00.51 prove
bounded quarantine and authorized resolution. The separate models do not yet
prove that every legal lifecycle path preserves one global resource total.

A runtime may implement each phase correctly in isolation while losing or
duplicating capacity at a phase boundary:

- available to held;
- held to consumed;
- held to quarantined;
- held back to available after proof of no effect;
- quarantined to consumed; or
- quarantined back to available.

The reservation record also needs a closed disposition relation. A consumed
reservation must not later become quarantined or released.

* Decision

The aggregate ledger has four partitions:

- available;
- held;
- consumed; and
- quarantined.

The conserved total is:

#+begin_quote
=available + held + consumed + quarantined=.
#+end_quote

Every legal transition moves an amount between exactly two partitions and
preserves the total.

Each reservation has one disposition:

- =held=;
- =consumed=;
- =quarantined=; or
- =released=.

=consumed= and =released= are terminal.

* Ledger transitions

The six legal aggregate movements are:

| Transition | Source | Destination |
|------------+--------+-------------|
| reserve | available | held |
| consume held | held | consumed |
| quarantine held | held | quarantined |
| release held | held | available |
| consume quarantined | quarantined | consumed |
| release quarantined | quarantined | available |

Each transition carries a proof that the source partition contains at least the
moved amount. Natural-number subtraction is never used as silent saturation.

* Global conservation theorem

For any legal =LedgerTransition before after=:

#+begin_quote
=total(after) = total(before)=.
#+end_quote

The theorem is proven once by case analysis over the closed transition type.
Future transition variants must extend the proof before entering the shared
schema.

* Reservation disposition

The legal per-reservation edges are:

#+begin_quote
=held -> consumed=

=held -> quarantined=

=held -> released=

=quarantined -> consumed=

=quarantined -> released=.
#+end_quote

There are no outgoing edges from =consumed= or =released=.

This prevents one linear lifecycle trace from both consuming and releasing the
same reservation.

* Revision discipline

Every committed reservation transition:

- preserves reservation identity and amount;
- changes disposition along a legal edge; and
- increments revision by one.

The transition proof does not by itself provide concurrent compare-and-swap.
RFC 00.53 will add the linearizability/CAS contract that ensures only one
transition from a shared revision commits.

* Example

Starting with 100 available units:

1. reserve 20: =80 available, 20 held=;
2. quarantine 20: =80 available, 20 quarantined=;
3. prove no effect and release: =100 available=.

Every intermediate total is 100.

The alternative completed path ends with =80 available, 20 consumed= and also
preserves total 100.

* Relationship to graph cost

Ledger units may represent tokens, money, provider quota, or another additive
resource. Each resource dimension uses its own conserved ledger.

Schedule span such as parallel rounds is not a conserved additive resource and
does not use this ledger algebra.

The route admission bundle binds the exact ledger dimension, amount, and
revision.

* Python Turbo Graph consequence

The future Python runtime SHOULD:

- use a closed ledger transition variant;
- validate source capacity before subtraction;
- atomically update the aggregate ledger and reservation record;
- verify total conservation at commit;
- make consumed/released records terminal; and
- include ledger revision in route admission receipts.

* Required Lean obligations

The companion Lean model MUST prove:

- each of the six movements preserves total capacity;
- every legal aggregate transition preserves total capacity;
- consumed and released dispositions are terminal;
- every record transition increments revision;
- the quarantine-release example preserves total 100; and
- the quarantine-consume example preserves total 100.

The modular audit emits these theorem families as Lean-generated JSON.

* Design finding

Local reservation and quarantine theorems did not establish conservation across
their boundary. The combined closed transition type makes every partition move
explicit and exposes concurrency as the remaining missing premise rather than
hiding it inside arithmetic.
