* Design findings

Lean and Typst exposed these protocol corrections:

1. =chooseBestGraphCandidate= required generic membership, feasibility, and
   lexicographic optimality theorems; concrete route examples were insufficient.
2. =pathValidated=true= is not a source-to-target =RankedDAG.Walk= witness.
3. A selected catalog entry must retain the closed costed trace and realization
   provenance even though selection operates on projected candidates.
4. Local selection becomes global only under snapshot-bound catalog
   completeness.
5. Duplicate projections provide existential, not unique, trace provenance.
6. Typst stdin evaluation rejected a reserved record field and unquoted
   multi-letter mathematical operators before this receipt was closed.

* Remaining proof boundary

The inspect scheduler frontier-closure receipt is not yet proved to construct
=RealizedTraceCatalogComplete=.  RFC 10.05.24 therefore states global
optimality conditionally on catalog completeness.
