* Verified claims

- Appending a route step adds exactly that step's uncached-token and round
  costs.
- A valid extension increases transition count by one and does not decrease
  the graph-hop lower bound.
- The partial-route lexicographic lower bound is monotone.
- The executable Boolean order is equivalent to the propositional order.
- The scheduler covers every input frontier with exactly one decision class.
- Every scheduled prune is safe for all candidates represented by the
  frontier.
- Every scheduled inspection is currently not prunable.
- An empty inspection list implies every input frontier was pruned.
- A completed schedule plus snapshot-bound coverage lifts visible optimality to
  global no-worse ordering.
- Allowing graph-hop lower bounds to decrease breaks extension monotonicity.

* Remaining boundary

The scheduler currently classifies frontiers against a fixed selected
candidate.  The next proof layer must model repeated inspection,
materialization, selected-candidate updates, and rescheduling under a decreasing
fuel measure.
