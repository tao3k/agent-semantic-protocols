* Lean-discovered RFC repairs

1. Complete-trace closure safety requires an admitted initial-state invariant.
2. Zero remaining mandatory work does not imply safe closure.
3. Productive steps decrease \(m+u\); terminal steps need not.
4. The general progress proposition is decrease-or-typed-terminal.
5. A mandatory-only budget can undercount unresolved discovery or graph work.
6. Bounded productive traces do not prove environmental retry fairness.

These repairs are normative in
=00.12-searchloop-trace-and-lean-json-audit.org= and explained in the embedded
Typst projection in =00.11-clause-first-mathematical-model.org=.
