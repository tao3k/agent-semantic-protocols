* Lean-discovered protocol correction

Lean proved that scheduler closure and candidate completeness are independent:

- an empty inspect frontier satisfies =ScheduleComplete=;
- the existing graph-only catalog still omits a feasible realized route;
- therefore =ScheduleComplete= does not imply =CandidateComplete=.

RFC 10.05.25 replaces full trace-catalog materialization with a certified
ledger containing:

- a snapshot-bound complete candidate universe;
- visible feasible realized trace entries;
- coverage by visible candidates or deferred certified frontiers;
- a successful selector;
- scheduler closure.

The global theorem retains full provenance for the selected trace without
requiring every safely pruned candidate to carry a materialized closed trace.

* Remaining boundary

The formal candidate universe is still a finite list of =GraphCandidate=
values.  A later proof must replace that list with a content-addressed or
symbolic universe commitment while preserving membership, coverage, snapshot,
and global optimality receipts.
