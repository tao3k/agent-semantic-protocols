* Executable Cost Receipt

The concrete routes prove:

| Route | Hops | Evidence | Raw | Uncached | Rounds |
|-
| CLI-style loop | 5 | 37 | 63 | 63 | 5 |
| Router plus materialization | 3 | 15 | 29 | 29 | 3 |
| Router plus model prefix hit | 3 | 15 | 29 | 17 | 3 |
| Router plus validated search miss | 4 | 25 | 45 | 29 | 4 |
| Router plus both cache hits | 3 | 7 | 21 | 9 | 3 |

The search cache and model cache are independently validated:

- semantic search caching changes the route and avoids repeated semantic work;
- model prefix caching preserves the logical route and raw tokens while
  reducing uncached prompt tokens;
- the joint route is admissible only when both receipts are independently
  sound.

Relative to a validated semantic-cache miss, the search hit reduces route
hops from 4 to 3, LLM rounds from 4 to 3, evidence tokens from 25 to 7, raw
tokens from 45 to 21, and uncached tokens from 29 to 9.

* Counterexamples

Lean rejects:

- a two-hop route that omits mandatory graph escalation;
- an admissible full-graph route that exceeds the token budget;
- a semantic search-cache hit without cache validation;
- a model cache receipt with cached prompt tokens greater than prompt tokens.

These counterexamples require the RFC to optimize over the feasible set rather
than all routes.
