#+startup: overview

* Scope

This receipt covers:

- lexicographic lower-bound transitivity;
- safe pruning of a certified deferred frontier;
- visible and deferred coverage of the complete candidate set;
- snapshot-bound acceptance of coverage receipts;
- global no-worse ordering without eager candidate materialization; and
- counterexamples for invalid bounds, missing coverage, and stale snapshots.

* Freshness observation

The parser-owned owner search used to open this proof iteration returned the
obsolete claim that =StepCatalogComplete= remained an external premise.  The
live RFC and Lean owner had already eliminated that premise.

This observation is not used as formal proof evidence.  It is the motivating
runtime counterexample for requiring a coverage receipt to match the current
source/index snapshot digest.

An initial Lean model still left the receipt digest independent of the covered
candidate list.  The snapshot premise was consequently unused by the
global-optimality proof.  The corrected model introduces =CandidateUniverse=
with both digest and complete candidates; =CoverageReceipt= is indexed by that
universe and proves its claimed digest equals the universe digest.

* Verified claims

- Lexicographic candidate-key ordering is transitive.
- A selected key that dominates a valid frontier lower bound dominates every
  candidate represented by that frontier.
- Visible optimality plus certified deferred-frontier coverage implies global
  no-worse ordering over the complete enumeration.
- A coverage receipt's claimed digest is proof-bound to the complete candidate
  universe digest.
- Snapshot acceptance is equivalent to equality between the current digest and
  that universe digest.
- The snapshot-bound theorem exposes snapshot acceptance as a protocol
  precondition for the lazy global-optimality result.
- An invalid lower bound can prune a better two-hop route behind a selected
  five-hop route.
- Visible optimality without coverage does not imply global optimality.
- A coverage receipt for snapshot =41= is rejected for snapshot =42=.

* Remaining boundary

The next proof layer must define lower bounds for partial routes and prove that
every route extension preserves or increases the lower-bound key.  After that,
an executable scheduler can be verified against the complete candidate
enumerator while exposing only a bounded =inspect= frontier.
