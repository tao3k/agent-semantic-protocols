* Proof boundary

The proof separates a safety-only trace relation from a liveness receipt.
Given =DecisionTotal= and a well-formed state, strong induction on remaining
loop work constructs a typed closed trace. The trace length is bounded by
initial work. With snapshot-bound coverage, its final selected candidate is
globally optimal over the candidate universe.

The proof does not derive provider availability, does not hide a typed provider
failure as successful closure, and does not claim that the constructed total
driver has minimum operational route cost.

* Lean-discovered RFC defects

1. =InspectLoopTrace.stop= permits a zero-step trace at a non-closed state.
   Trace existence is therefore not liveness.
2. Strict work decrease proves boundedness only after a next valid decision is
   available. =DecisionTotal= is an independent protocol premise.
3. A total driver can reach a globally optimal candidate without being the
   cheapest driver. Cost-optimality requires a separate trace-to-SearchRoute
   lifting theorem.
