** Verified obligations

1. =joinOutcome= is commutative, associative, and idempotent.
2. Results admitted into one indexed validation domain commute under normalized
   ledger merge.
3. Replaying one admitted result is idempotent.
4. =mergeMany= is invariant under every finite =List.Perm= of admitted results.
5. Raw writes to distinct clause keys commute.
6. Pair merge is commutative and idempotent after identity admission.
7. Clause and validation-domain mismatches produce typed conflicts.
8. Last-write-wins is order-dependent for conflicting outcomes on one clause.
9. Contradictory evidence dominates pending evidence under normalized join.

The fourth obligation closes the RFC's previous open claim “prove merge
confluence for independent clause-result orderings.”  It is stronger than a
single two-update example because it quantifies over finite permutations.

1. universal SearchLoop termination;
2. graph-escalation completeness;
3. provider evidence soundness;
4. scheduling fairness; or
5. confluence of arbitrary side effects.

validation domain.
