* Proof boundary

The proof binds one operational cost receipt to every inspect-loop decision,
projects the costed trace into =SearchRoute= and =GraphCandidate=, proves exact
token/round/transition fidelity, and proves separate accounting equations for
semantic-search cache savings and model prefix-cache savings.

It does not claim a complete catalog of total-driver traces, full
=admissibleB= for a route requirement, or global driver-cost optimality.

* Lean-discovered RFC defects

1. Semantic-cache identity must be bound to
   =RouteStep.kind = semanticCacheHit=; a free Boolean is insufficient.
2. Loop closure does not imply graph-path validation. Projection needs an
   independent path-validation receipt.
3. Total uncached tokens combine actual evidence and uncached prompt tokens.
   Equal totals do not identify whether semantic-search cache or model
   prefix-cache produced the saving.
4. Cost fidelity does not imply catalog completeness or optimizer globality.
   Those remain the next proof layer.
