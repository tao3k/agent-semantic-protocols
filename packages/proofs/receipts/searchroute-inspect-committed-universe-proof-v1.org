* Lean-discovered protocol corrections

- A root digest alone does not define a candidate set.
- Equal root digests do not imply equal membership predicates without a
  verifier and binding scheme.
- Exclusion completeness must be restricted to valid roots.
- Successful membership and exclusion verification both prove root validity.
- Verified membership and exclusion cannot both hold for the same root and
  candidate.
- Symbolic committed frontiers prove safe pruning without carrying candidate
  lists.
- Committed coverage and commitment completeness replace the explicit finite
  candidate universe in the global-driver theorem.
- The selected candidate retains a visible closed trace, realization, and
  cache provenance.

* Remaining boundary

The abstract =CandidateCommitmentScheme= requires a concrete authenticated
finite construction that binds canonical candidate encoding, algorithm
identity, root calculation, snapshot identity, membership and exclusion
verification, and deterministic receipt serialization.  RFC 10.05.27 and
=ASPProof.SearchRouteInspectFiniteCommitment= provide the ideal finite
correctness baseline; logarithmic authenticated openings remain future work.
