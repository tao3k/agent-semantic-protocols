* Logic defects found by Lean and Typst

The RFC was changed in response to proof pressure:

1. =witnessSet= and =ValidationIdentity.sourceSnapshot= are independent Lean
   fields.  The semantic key is therefore a flattened twelve-component tuple,
   not the previous eleven-component sketch.
2. =CacheState.workspaceGeneration= is reconciliation state, not part of the
   semantic witness.  Lean proves a whole-generation predicate can reject a
   still-valid semantic key after unrelated drift.
3. =ModelJudgmentKey= extends =SemanticKey=.  Model/prompt drift invalidates L4
   judgments while preserving parser-owned semantic reuse.
4. Selector drift is stale without an explicit relocation transition.
   Relocation is valid only after rekeying yields the complete current identity.
5. Typed diagnostics are derived from mismatch; their precedence cannot grant a
   cache hit.
6. The original two-scope/fourteen-declaration audit was incomplete.  The
   converged surface is 18 declarations admitted through five scopes.
7. Typst stdin compilation rejected the nonexistent =arrow.not.long= modifier.
   The embedded model now states the non-implication explicitly.
8. A draft Typst formula claimed \(v \ne s\).  Lean only justifies independent
   fields and semantic roles; their underlying encodings may coincide.  The
   model now forbids projection collapse without asserting value inequality.
