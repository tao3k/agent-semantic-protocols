* Verified claims

- The recursive endpoint enumerator emits only valid graph walks.
- Every walk whose hop count is within fuel appears in the enumerator output.
- =maxRank - rank(source)= is sufficient fuel for every ranked-DAG walk from
  the source.
- Every step list within =maxTransitions= is enumerated when its steps occur in
  the finite step-instance catalog.
- Every step in a feasible route is bounded by the route totals; cache
  soundness additionally bounds =cachedPromptTokens= by =promptTokens=.
- The finite =StepKind= enumeration and numeric budget ranges construct a step
  catalog satisfying =StepCatalogComplete=.
- Feasible route metadata is covered by four Boolean variants after the
  requirement fixes domain, closure digest, and closure state.
- Filtering bounded route variants with =feasibleB= constructs a complete
  route catalog under =StepCatalogComplete=.
- The graph-walk and constructed route catalogs jointly generate a complete
  graph-candidate list.
- Graph-walk completeness alone does not imply graph-candidate completeness.
- The old token and round budget admits routes with arbitrarily many zero-cost
  transitions.
- =GraphRouteBudget.maxTransitions= rejects routes that exceed the explicit
  transition ceiling.

* Open boundary

The finite step-instance and route catalogs are now constructed without an
external completeness premise.  The next proof layer must prove that a lazy
branch-and-bound frontier preserves the complete enumerator's optimum and
coverage receipt without materializing the full Cartesian product.  Route
normalization must also preserve feasibility, cache soundness, realization,
and optimizer cost.
